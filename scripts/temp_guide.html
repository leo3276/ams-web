AMS (Accounting Made Simple)

Complete Business Capabilities, System Architecture & User Guide
The All-in-One Dual-Platform Financial Operating System for Growing Businesses

1. Executive Summary

AMS (Accounting Made Simple) is an enterprise-grade financial management and bookkeeping ecosystem designed specifically for SMEs, entrepreneurs, and fast-growing businesses.

Historically, business owners have faced a frustrating dilemma: either use overly complicated corporate accounting software requiring specialized accounting degrees, or rely on manual paper notebooks and scattered Excel sheets that lead to tax non-compliance, uncollected debts, and cash flow blind spots.

AMS resolves this permanently through a dual-platform ecosystem:

  • The Native Mobile App (ams-app): Fast, frictionless, on-the-go data capture powered by Vision AI OCR, receipt photo scanning, verbal financial situation reporting, and instant WhatsApp customer billing.
  • The Companion Web Application (ams-web): A powerful desktop workstation featuring keyboard-accelerated spreadsheet bookkeeping, comprehensive stock valuation radars, continuous all-in-one financial statements, and certified accountant audit hubs.

2. Mobile App Capabilities (ams-app)

👁️ 1. Vision AI OCR & Document Snapping Engine

  • Camera & Photo Library Scanning: Take a live photo of any paper receipt, invoice, fuel slip, or utility bill, or upload PDF documents directly from your device.
  • Claude 3.5 Sonnet Vision Intelligence: Extracts vendor name, date, total amount, payment channel, and auto-classifies the transaction into proper accounting categories (Revenue, Cost of Goods, Operating Expense, Fixed Asset, Liability).
  • Inventory Restock Scanning: Snaps wholesale restock invoices, automatically parses itemized lines (product names, quantities, unit costs), updates stock counts in the catalog, and logs the restocking expense in the general ledger.

🛡️ 2. Safe-to-Spend Liquidity Radar & Cash Runway Forecast

  • Shielded Spendable Cash: Calculates real spendable money by automatically deducting an estimated 15% Tax Reserve and a 7-day Operating Overhead Buffer from total cash and bank balances.
  • 30-Day Cash Runway: Projects the number of days the business can survive without new sales based on current daily operating burn rates.
  • Trapped Capital Radar: Highlights uncollected customer receivables and tied-up stock capital.

🎙️ 3. Verbal Financial Situation Narrative & AI CFO Story

  • Human-like Situation Reports: Converts dry numbers into an articulate, conversational executive story explaining revenue momentum, largest spending culprits (e.g. "Fuel took 42% of spending"), liquidity cushions, and prioritized action items.
  • 1-Tap Share Sheet: Exports the narrative situation brief directly to WhatsApp or Email.

💼 4. 5-Module Accountant & Audit Hub

  • Continuous 4-Report Snapshot: P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Trial Balance in one continuous scrollable view.
  • 4 Data Quality Flags: Unsent draft invoices, generic categories, un-depreciated fixed assets, and Trial Balance gap explanation.
  • Receivables & Debt Aging Ledger: Open invoices sorted by most overdue first (daysOverdue ↓).
  • Monthly Period Closing & Lock Controls: Lock historical months to protect verified books from accidental alterations.

🏛️ 5. Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) Tax Filing Preparation

  • Entity Classification: Support for Sole Proprietorship (Progressive Bands) and Corporate (25% Flat Rate).
  • Statutory GRA Tax Bracket Table: Slice-by-slice calculation across all 7 statutory GRA tax bands (0% → 35%) with effective tax rate %.
  • Filing Deadline Countdown: Live countdown with overdue alerts and 1-tap schedule sharing.

3. Companion Web Portal Capabilities (ams-web)

📊 1. Executive Financial Dashboard (/dashboard)

  • Safe-to-Spend Hero Banner: Real-time available liquidity with automated tax reserves and weekly overhead buffers.
  • Cash Runway & Trapped Capital Widgets: Live runway in days alongside uncollected customer debt and tied-up stock capital.
  • Core Monthly KPI Grid: Revenue, Cost of Goods (COGS), Operating Expenses (OpEx), and Net Profit with gross margin %.

🧾 2. Full Invoicing & Billing Studio (/invoices)

  • Interactive Line Item Builder: Real-time Inventory Item Picker (auto-fills prices and displays available stock).
  • Printable Branded PDF Layout: Clean, formatted invoice ready for browser "Print / Save as PDF" (window.print()).
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Chasing Links & 1-Tap Mark Paid Ledger Sync.

📋 3. Spreadsheet Fast-Entry Bookkeeping & General Ledger (/bookkeeping)

  • Spreadsheet Fast-Entry Grid: Type in the top row and press Tab to move across columns; entries automatically save to the database on blur.
  • Valuation Summary Strip: Real-time Total Revenue Inflows, Total Outflows, and Net Cash Movement.
  • Search & Filter Pills and 1-Click CSV Ledger Export.

📦 4. Inventory Management & Stock Valuation Radar (/inventory)

  • Stock Valuation Radar: Live Total Cost Valuation, Retail Valuation, Potential Gross Profit, and Stock Health indicators.
  • Real-time Margin Column: Displays profit-per-unit and margin % for every catalog product with 1-click CSV export.

📈 5. Financial Reports & Statements (/reports)

  • Continuous All-in-One Accountant Sheet: View all four statements (P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Trial Balance) in one continuous view.
  • Period Presets (Month, Quarter, Year) & 1-Click Brief Export.

💼 6. Web Accountant Dashboard (/accountant) & Tax Prep (/tax)

  • Reconciliation Sanity Radar: Debits vs credits sanity check (Δ = 0 balance indicator).
  • Debt Aging Table and GRA Tax Schedule Computation Table.
